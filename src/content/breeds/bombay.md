## History & Origin

The Bombay originated in United States. This distinctive breed has captured the hearts of cat lovers with its unique combination of striking appearance and engaging personality.

Whether prized for working abilities, stunning looks, or wonderful temperament, the Bombay has established itself as a uncommon breed with a devoted following among cat enthusiasts worldwide.

## Appearance & Physical Traits

The Bombay displays a beautiful short coat characterized by short, fine, satiny with patent-leather shine. The breed features black coloring with solid patterns, complemented by copper eyes that give this breed its distinctive and captivating expression.

The coat is one of the breed's most defining features — short, fine, satiny with patent-leather shine. This contributes significantly to the breed's overall appearance and care requirements.

## Personality & Temperament

The Bombay is a moderately active companion with an engaging personality. They form strong bonds with their families and are very affectionate with those they trust. These cats are excellent with children and other pets.

With high intelligence, these cats enjoy interactive play and puzzle toys. Their vocalization level is moderate, meaning they communicate as needed.

They strike a nice balance between playfulness and relaxation.

## Care & Grooming Guide

The Bombay requires minimal grooming. A weekly brushing with a soft brush or grooming glove is sufficient to remove loose hair and keep the coat glossy.

Shedding Level: 2/5. Grooming Needs: 1/5.

Beyond coat care, these cats need standard feline maintenance: regular nail trimming, dental care, and a clean litter box. Provide scratching posts and climbing structures to satisfy natural instincts.

## Health & Lifespan

The Bombay typically enjoys a lifespan of 12-16 years. With proper care, regular veterinary checkups, a high-quality diet, and an indoor lifestyle, many individuals live toward the upper end of this range.

Like all breeds, the Bombay may be predisposed to certain health conditions. Reputable breeders screen for known genetic issues. Maintaining a healthy weight, providing dental care, and keeping vaccinations current all contribute to a long, healthy life.

## Diet & Nutrition

Feed your Bombay a high-quality, protein-rich cat food appropriate for their life stage. Portion control is important to prevent obesity. Always provide fresh, clean water. Some cats enjoy puzzle feeders that make mealtime mentally enriching.

## Exercise Needs

The Bombay benefits from daily interactive play sessions. Wand toys, laser pointers, and fetch games are great options. A cat tree by a window provides entertainment throughout the day.
