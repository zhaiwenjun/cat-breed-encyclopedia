## History & Origin

The Burmese originated in Burma (Myanmar) / Thailand. This distinctive breed has captured the hearts of cat lovers with its unique combination of striking appearance and engaging personality.

Whether prized for working abilities, stunning looks, or wonderful temperament, the Burmese has established itself as a uncommon breed with a devoted following among cat enthusiasts worldwide.

## Appearance & Physical Traits

The Burmese displays a beautiful short coat characterized by short, fine, glossy, and satin-like with almost no undercoat. The breed features sable coloring with solid patterns, complemented by gold eyes that give this breed its distinctive and captivating expression.

The coat is one of the breed's most defining features — short, fine, glossy, and satin-like with almost no undercoat. This contributes significantly to the breed's overall appearance and care requirements.

## Personality & Temperament

The Burmese is a moderately active companion with an engaging personality. They form strong bonds with their families and are very affectionate with those they trust. These cats are excellent with children and other pets.

With high intelligence, these cats enjoy interactive play and puzzle toys. Their vocalization level is moderate, meaning they communicate as needed.

They strike a nice balance between playfulness and relaxation.

## Care & Grooming Guide

The Burmese requires minimal grooming. A weekly brushing with a soft brush or grooming glove is sufficient to remove loose hair and keep the coat glossy.

Shedding Level: 1/5. Grooming Needs: 1/5.

Beyond coat care, these cats need standard feline maintenance: regular nail trimming, dental care, and a clean litter box. Provide scratching posts and climbing structures to satisfy natural instincts.

## Health & Lifespan

The Burmese typically enjoys a lifespan of 12-18 years. With proper care, regular veterinary checkups, a high-quality diet, and an indoor lifestyle, many individuals live toward the upper end of this range.

Like all breeds, the Burmese may be predisposed to certain health conditions. Reputable breeders screen for known genetic issues. Maintaining a healthy weight, providing dental care, and keeping vaccinations current all contribute to a long, healthy life.

## Diet & Nutrition

Feed your Burmese a high-quality, protein-rich cat food appropriate for their life stage. Portion control is important to prevent obesity. Always provide fresh, clean water. Some cats enjoy puzzle feeders that make mealtime mentally enriching.

## Exercise Needs

The Burmese benefits from daily interactive play sessions. Wand toys, laser pointers, and fetch games are great options. A cat tree by a window provides entertainment throughout the day.
