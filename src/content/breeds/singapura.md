## History & Origin

The Singapura originated in Singapore. This distinctive breed has captured the hearts of cat lovers with its unique combination of striking appearance and engaging personality.

Whether prized for working abilities, stunning looks, or wonderful temperament, the Singapura has established itself as a rare breed with a devoted following among cat enthusiasts worldwide.

## Appearance & Physical Traits

The Singapura displays a beautiful short coat characterized by very short, fine, silky, and close-lying. The breed features sepia agouti (ticked) coloring with ticked tabby patterns, complemented by hazel eyes that give this breed its distinctive and captivating expression.

The coat is one of the breed's most defining features — very short, fine, silky, and close-lying. This contributes significantly to the breed's overall appearance and care requirements.

## Personality & Temperament

The Singapura is a highly energetic companion with an engaging personality. They form strong bonds with their families and are very affectionate with those they trust. These cats are excellent with children and other pets.

With high intelligence, these cats enjoy interactive play and puzzle toys. Their vocalization level is moderate, meaning they communicate as needed.

The breed thrives in active households that can provide plenty of playtime and attention.

## Care & Grooming Guide

The Singapura requires minimal grooming. A weekly brushing with a soft brush or grooming glove is sufficient to remove loose hair and keep the coat glossy.

Shedding Level: 1/5. Grooming Needs: 1/5.

Beyond coat care, these cats need standard feline maintenance: regular nail trimming, dental care, and a clean litter box. Provide scratching posts and climbing structures to satisfy natural instincts.

## Health & Lifespan

The Singapura typically enjoys a lifespan of 11-15 years. With proper care, regular veterinary checkups, a high-quality diet, and an indoor lifestyle, many individuals live toward the upper end of this range.

Like all breeds, the Singapura may be predisposed to certain health conditions. Reputable breeders screen for known genetic issues. Maintaining a healthy weight, providing dental care, and keeping vaccinations current all contribute to a long, healthy life.

## Diet & Nutrition

Feed your Singapura a high-quality, protein-rich cat food appropriate for their life stage. Portion control is important to prevent obesity. Always provide fresh, clean water. Some cats enjoy puzzle feeders that make mealtime mentally enriching.

## Exercise Needs

The Singapura needs plenty of physical activity and mental stimulation. Provide cat trees, interactive toys, puzzle feeders, and dedicated play sessions daily. Without adequate stimulation, they may become bored or destructive.
