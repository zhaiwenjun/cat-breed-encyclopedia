## History & Origin

The Selkirk Rex originated in United States. This distinctive breed has captured the hearts of cat lovers with its unique combination of striking appearance and engaging personality.

Whether prized for working abilities, stunning looks, or wonderful temperament, the Selkirk Rex has established itself as a uncommon breed with a devoted following among cat enthusiasts worldwide.

## Appearance & Physical Traits

The Selkirk Rex displays a beautiful short coat characterized by curly, plush, and soft; dense with loose, individual ringlets. The breed features all colors and patterns coloring with solid patterns, complemented by all colors eyes that give this breed its distinctive and captivating expression.

The coat is one of the breed's most defining features — curly, plush, and soft; dense with loose, individual ringlets. This contributes significantly to the breed's overall appearance and care requirements.

## Personality & Temperament

The Selkirk Rex is a moderately active companion with an engaging personality. They form strong bonds with their families and are very affectionate with those they trust. These cats are excellent with children and other pets.

With high intelligence, these cats enjoy interactive play and puzzle toys. Their vocalization level is low, meaning they tend to be quiet and soft-spoken.

They strike a nice balance between playfulness and relaxation.

## Care & Grooming Guide

The Selkirk Rex requires minimal grooming. A weekly brushing with a soft brush or grooming glove is sufficient to remove loose hair and keep the coat glossy.

Shedding Level: 2/5. Grooming Needs: 2/5.

Beyond coat care, these cats need standard feline maintenance: regular nail trimming, dental care, and a clean litter box. Provide scratching posts and climbing structures to satisfy natural instincts.

## Health & Lifespan

The Selkirk Rex typically enjoys a lifespan of 10-15 years. With proper care, regular veterinary checkups, a high-quality diet, and an indoor lifestyle, many individuals live toward the upper end of this range.

Like all breeds, the Selkirk Rex may be predisposed to certain health conditions. Reputable breeders screen for known genetic issues. Maintaining a healthy weight, providing dental care, and keeping vaccinations current all contribute to a long, healthy life.

## Diet & Nutrition

Feed your Selkirk Rex a high-quality, protein-rich cat food appropriate for their life stage. Portion control is important to prevent obesity. Always provide fresh, clean water. Some cats enjoy puzzle feeders that make mealtime mentally enriching.

## Exercise Needs

The Selkirk Rex benefits from daily interactive play sessions. Wand toys, laser pointers, and fetch games are great options. A cat tree by a window provides entertainment throughout the day.
