## History & Origin

The Highlander originated in United States. This distinctive breed has captured the hearts of cat lovers with its unique combination of striking appearance and engaging personality.

Whether prized for working abilities, stunning looks, or wonderful temperament, the Highlander has established itself as a very rare breed with a devoted following among cat enthusiasts worldwide.

## Appearance & Physical Traits

The Highlander displays a beautiful short coat characterized by short to medium; dense and resilient. The breed features all colors and patterns coloring with tabby patterns, complemented by all colors eyes that give this breed its distinctive and captivating expression.

The coat is one of the breed's most defining features — short to medium; dense and resilient. This contributes significantly to the breed's overall appearance and care requirements.

## Personality & Temperament

The Highlander is a highly energetic companion with an engaging personality. They form strong bonds with their families and are very affectionate with those they trust. These cats are excellent with children and other pets.

With high intelligence, these cats enjoy interactive play and puzzle toys. Their vocalization level is low, meaning they tend to be quiet and soft-spoken.

The breed thrives in active households that can provide plenty of playtime and attention.

## Care & Grooming Guide

The Highlander requires minimal grooming. A weekly brushing with a soft brush or grooming glove is sufficient to remove loose hair and keep the coat glossy.

Shedding Level: 3/5. Grooming Needs: 2/5.

Beyond coat care, these cats need standard feline maintenance: regular nail trimming, dental care, and a clean litter box. Provide scratching posts and climbing structures to satisfy natural instincts.

## Health & Lifespan

The Highlander typically enjoys a lifespan of 10-15 years. With proper care, regular veterinary checkups, a high-quality diet, and an indoor lifestyle, many individuals live toward the upper end of this range.

Like all breeds, the Highlander may be predisposed to certain health conditions. Reputable breeders screen for known genetic issues. Maintaining a healthy weight, providing dental care, and keeping vaccinations current all contribute to a long, healthy life.

## Diet & Nutrition

Feed your Highlander a high-quality, protein-rich cat food appropriate for their life stage. Portion control is important to prevent obesity. Always provide fresh, clean water. Some cats enjoy puzzle feeders that make mealtime mentally enriching.

## Exercise Needs

The Highlander needs plenty of physical activity and mental stimulation. Provide cat trees, interactive toys, puzzle feeders, and dedicated play sessions daily. Without adequate stimulation, they may become bored or destructive.
