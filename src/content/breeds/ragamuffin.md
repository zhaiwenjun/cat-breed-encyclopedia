## History & Origin

The Ragamuffin originated in United States. This distinctive breed has captured the hearts of cat lovers with its unique combination of striking appearance and engaging personality.

Whether prized for working abilities, stunning looks, or wonderful temperament, the Ragamuffin has established itself as a rare breed with a devoted following among cat enthusiasts worldwide.

## Appearance & Physical Traits

The Ragamuffin displays a beautiful long coat characterized by medium-long, dense, silky, and plush; rabbit-like texture. The breed features all colors and patterns coloring with solid patterns, complemented by all colors eyes that give this breed its distinctive and captivating expression.

The coat is one of the breed's most defining features — medium-long, dense, silky, and plush; rabbit-like texture. This contributes significantly to the breed's overall appearance and care requirements.

## Personality & Temperament

The Ragamuffin is a calm and relaxed companion with an engaging personality. They form strong bonds with their families and are very affectionate with those they trust. These cats are excellent with children and other pets.

With high intelligence, these cats enjoy interactive play and puzzle toys. Their vocalization level is low, meaning they tend to be quiet and soft-spoken.

They enjoy being lap cats and calm companionship.

## Care & Grooming Guide

The Ragamuffin requires daily brushing to prevent mats and tangles. Regular professional grooming every 4-6 weeks is recommended. Pay special attention to ruff, belly, and tail areas.

Shedding Level: 4/5. Grooming Needs: 3/5.

Beyond coat care, these cats need standard feline maintenance: regular nail trimming, dental care, and a clean litter box. Provide scratching posts and climbing structures to satisfy natural instincts.

## Health & Lifespan

The Ragamuffin typically enjoys a lifespan of 12-16 years. With proper care, regular veterinary checkups, a high-quality diet, and an indoor lifestyle, many individuals live toward the upper end of this range.

Like all breeds, the Ragamuffin may be predisposed to certain health conditions. Reputable breeders screen for known genetic issues. Maintaining a healthy weight, providing dental care, and keeping vaccinations current all contribute to a long, healthy life.

## Diet & Nutrition

Feed your Ragamuffin a high-quality, protein-rich cat food appropriate for their life stage. Portion control is important to prevent obesity. Always provide fresh, clean water. Some cats enjoy puzzle feeders that make mealtime mentally enriching.

## Exercise Needs

The Ragamuffin has relatively low exercise needs. Short daily play sessions with wand toys or balls are sufficient. They enjoy lounging in sunny spots and watching the world from a window perch.

## Is This Breed Right For You?

**Pros:**
