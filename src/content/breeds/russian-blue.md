## History & Origin

The Russian Blue originated in Russia (Arkhangelsk). This distinctive breed has captured the hearts of cat lovers with its unique combination of striking appearance and engaging personality.

Whether prized for working abilities, stunning looks, or wonderful temperament, the Russian Blue has established itself as a uncommon breed with a devoted following among cat enthusiasts worldwide.

## Appearance & Physical Traits

The Russian Blue displays a beautiful short coat characterized by short, dense, plush double coat with silver tipping creating shimmer. The breed features blue-gray with silver tips coloring with solid patterns, complemented by vivid green eyes that give this breed its distinctive and captivating expression.

The coat is one of the breed's most defining features — short, dense, plush double coat with silver tipping creating shimmer. This contributes significantly to the breed's overall appearance and care requirements.

## Personality & Temperament

The Russian Blue is a moderately active companion with an engaging personality. They form strong bonds with their families and are moderately affectionate with those they trust. These cats are good with respectful children.

With high intelligence, these cats enjoy interactive play and puzzle toys. Their vocalization level is low, meaning they tend to be quiet and soft-spoken.

They strike a nice balance between playfulness and relaxation.

## Care & Grooming Guide

The Russian Blue requires minimal grooming. A weekly brushing with a soft brush or grooming glove is sufficient to remove loose hair and keep the coat glossy.

Shedding Level: 2/5. Grooming Needs: 2/5.

Beyond coat care, these cats need standard feline maintenance: regular nail trimming, dental care, and a clean litter box. Provide scratching posts and climbing structures to satisfy natural instincts.

## Health & Lifespan

The Russian Blue typically enjoys a lifespan of 15-20 years. With proper care, regular veterinary checkups, a high-quality diet, and an indoor lifestyle, many individuals live toward the upper end of this range.

Like all breeds, the Russian Blue may be predisposed to certain health conditions. Reputable breeders screen for known genetic issues. Maintaining a healthy weight, providing dental care, and keeping vaccinations current all contribute to a long, healthy life.

## Diet & Nutrition

Feed your Russian Blue a high-quality, protein-rich cat food appropriate for their life stage. Portion control is important to prevent obesity. Always provide fresh, clean water. Some cats enjoy puzzle feeders that make mealtime mentally enriching.

## Exercise Needs

The Russian Blue benefits from daily interactive play sessions. Wand toys, laser pointers, and fetch games are great options. A cat tree by a window provides entertainment throughout the day.
