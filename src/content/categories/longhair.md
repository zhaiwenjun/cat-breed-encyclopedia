Longhair cats are some of the most visually stunning felines in the world. Their flowing coats come in a variety of colors and patterns, and many longhair breeds are known for their calm, affectionate temperaments.

## What Defines a Longhair Cat?

Longhair cats carry a recessive gene that produces longer, thicker fur. These breeds often have distinctive ruffs around the neck, tufted ears, and plumed tails. The coat length provides insulation and gives these cats their majestic appearance.

## Grooming Requirements

Longhair cats require dedicated grooming routines:

- **Daily brushing** to prevent mats and tangles
- **Regular bathing** every 4-6 weeks to keep the coat clean
- **Sanitary trims** to prevent litter from sticking to fur
- **Professional grooming** may be needed for some breeds

## Popular Longhair Breeds

The most beloved longhair breeds include the Persian, known for its flat face and calm demeanor; the Maine Coon, the gentle giant of the cat world; the Ragdoll, famous for going limp when held; the Norwegian Forest Cat, a hardy natural breed; and the Birman, with its striking blue eyes and white gloves.
