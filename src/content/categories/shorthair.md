Shorthair cats make up the vast majority of domestic cat breeds. Their coats require less maintenance than longhair breeds, but they come in an incredible variety of colors, patterns, and body types.

## Why Choose a Shorthair Cat?

- **Lower maintenance** grooming compared to longhair breeds
- **Wide variety** of personalities, from couch potatoes to active athletes
- **Heat tolerant** — better suited for warmer climates
- **Shedding is manageable** with weekly brushing

## Coat Patterns in Shorthairs

Shorthair breeds showcase nearly every feline coat pattern: solid, tabby (classic, mackerel, spotted, ticked), bicolor, calico, tortoiseshell, colorpoint, and more.
